About the house

A house built for the ritual between plate and pause.

Aureum began with a simple idea: refine the objects that accompany fine hospitality. The result is a toothpick collection that feels less disposable and more curated—precise, tactile, and beautifully consequential.

Craft

The language is restrained but never plain.

Every finish, shadow line, and accent is selected to create contrast without clutter. The house is built around subtle drama and the confidence of restraint.

Material language: matte black, oxblood red, and burnished gold used with surgical clarity.
Atmosphere: the table is framed as a moment of theatre, not just a place to eat.
Method

Made to bridge course, conversation, and memory.

The reserve line is not loud. It arrives as a precise gesture, one that sharpens the mood of a dinner and makes every host moment feel considered.

Small-batch finishing and exacting quality control for every release.
Designed for private homes, hospitality groups, and gifting moments that deserve ceremony.
