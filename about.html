About the house

A house built for the ritual between a plate and a pause.

Aureum began with a simple proposition: refine the objects that accompany high-end hospitality. The result is a toothpick collection that feels less like a disposable afterthought and more like a crafted finishing detail—direct, elegant, and full of atmosphere.

Craft

The design language is restrained, but never plain.

Every upright profile, every siren-red accent, and every darkened finish is intended to mimic the presence of a luxury object: subtle, confident, and instantly recognizable the moment it hits the table.

Material philosophy: matte, satin, and smoked finishes selected to read as tactile rather than decorative.
Sensory philosophy: pleasure is measured through tone, aroma, and the quiet drama of presentation.
Method

Made to sit between course and conversation.

The signature set is not meant to shout. Instead, it frames a dining experience with better pacing, stronger sensory recall, and a polished house note that feels distinctly high-end.

Small-batch finishing with exacting quality control for every collector release.
Designed for refined homes, hospitality groups, and gifting moments that require memorable detail.
